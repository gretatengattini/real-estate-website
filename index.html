Get to know me

I grew up in a small town in northern Italy and studied law and sustainability before moving to the United States. At 25, I was awarded a scholarship to complete my master's thesis at Columbia, and later earned a second master's in business analytics in San Francisco.

Along the way, I built and ran a business reselling luxury accessories, working directly with private clients in highly competitive markets. That experience shaped my understanding of negotiation, value assessment, and how small details influence major financial decisions.

I later worked in tech, where I learned to think in systems, data, and structure. But I was ultimately drawn to a path where strategy and human impact intersect — which led me to real estate.

Today, I combine legal training, data-driven analysis, and real-world negotiation experience to guide buyers and sellers through complex decisions with clarity and precision. My approach is structured, responsive, and honest about trade-offs — so you can move forward confidently, knowing you're fully supported.
